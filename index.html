How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form below, send us a DM on Instagram, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm your slot as soon as possible.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes! We recommend booking your bridal trial 4–8 weeks before the big day so we can perfect your look together.

Which products do you use?

We use premium, skin-friendly professional products and always adapt to sensitive skin. Let us know about any allergies when booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule or cancel, so we can offer the slot to another client.

Do you have gift vouchers?

Absolutely — treat someone you love to a little bliss. Message us to arrange a voucher for any treatment or amount.
